# Open Source Assessment Guide

This guide will help you complete the open source self-assessment for your organization and establish program goals.

## Step 1: Complete the Self-Assessment

1. Visit the [Open Source Self-Assessment Tool](https://githubtraining.github.io/oss-assessment/)
2. Complete each of the three assessments:
   - Consuming open source software
   - Contributing back to open source projects
   - Producing open source software
3. For each dimension, note your current level (Ad-hoc, Managed, Defined, Measured, or Optimized)
4. Record your results in the [Open Source Self-Assessment](open-source-assessment.md) document

## Step 2: Establish Program Goals

1. For each dimension, determine a target level you want to achieve
2. Consider your organization's priorities and resources when setting these goals
3. Update the Goals column in the assessment document
4. Remember that goals should be:
   - Realistic and achievable
   - Aligned with your organization's broader objectives
   - Measurable so you can track progress

## Step 3: Develop an Action Plan

1. For each dimension, identify specific actions needed to reach your target level
2. Consider the gap between your current level and target level
3. Prioritize actions based on impact and feasibility
4. Document your action plan in the assessment document

## Step 4: Review and Refine

1. Share the assessment and goals with key stakeholders
2. Gather feedback and refine as needed
3. Establish a timeline for reviewing progress
4. Update the document as your organization evolves

Remember, this is a living document that should be revisited regularly as your open source program matures.
